## 2. Overall Final Website Readiness Decision

### **FINAL VERDICT: MAJOR ISSUES (NOT APPROVED FOR PRODUCTION LAUNCH; READY FOR STAGING REMEDIATION)**

**Justification:**  
Per Phase 20 rules (*"A website must not receive a production-ready decision if critical security, data integrity, customer access, or operational issues remain unresolved"*), the platform **cannot** be approved for live production deployment today due to three P0 blockers:
1. **Supply-Chain Security (`SEC-F01`):** 25 known vulnerabilities in locked Composer packages (Laravel 9.52 / Symfony 6.0 / DomPDF 2.0.8), including 1 Critical and 6 High CVEs.
2. **Legal, Contact & Marketing Truthfulness (`LEG-01`, `LEG-02`, `BUG-03`):** Placeholder contact emails/addresses (`techsupport.com`, `techsupportsolutions.com`, `123 Tech Avenue`, `Cyber Tower One`, 6 global skyscraper offices) and unverified certification/infrastructure claims (`Tier IV data centers`, `24/7 Global SOC`, `ISO 27001 / SOC 2 Type II`) across public pages.
3. **20-Role AI Employee Workforce Gap (`BUG-02`):** The required 20-role autonomous AI Employee Workforce is not implemented in the backend (only 20 chatbot skills and 9 commercial service pages exist).

At the same time, the **core engineering foundation** — authentication, MFA/OTP, RBAC, customer tenant isolation, ticketing, SLA engine, single-ledger multi-currency finance, FWallet, PDF generation, AI support assistant with 20 deterministic skills, Knowledge Hub (41 articles), and encrypted backup system — is **genuinely implemented and passes 169/169 executed automated tests**. Once Phase B blockers are resolved, the platform can immediately advance to **READY FOR PRODUCTION REVIEW**.
